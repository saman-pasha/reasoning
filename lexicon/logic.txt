# lexicon/logic.txt -- the second measure (TRANSLATOR-PLAN.md, since
# reasoning 1.69.0): English sentences and the Cocolog each says, written
# by hand, one a line, the sentence and its Cocolog apart by a tab. The
# Cocolog is compared as a logic form (cocolog_read/2), so its layout and
# its variables names are free, and a reserved name may be written either
# way (sleep(john) or is_sleep(john)). A line beginning # is a comment.
#
