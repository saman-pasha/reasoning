## What the matrix says

- **One task decides every mode comparison.** `lcs/plain` fails its
  held-out case 3/3; every obligation mode passes it 3/3. The gain of
  `derive` is the whole result.
- **The upper modes are indistinguishable here.** `contract`, `extract`
  and `full` add no correctness over `derive` on this trio. Their paired
  intervals are exactly zero, because every task is 3/3 in both arms.
- **`extract` buys nothing for a clear price.** It costs 9 489 tokens
  against `contract`'s 7 306 and `derive`'s 7 050 for the same 9/9. The
  extraction call is the difference.
- **`full` is `contract` plus no measurable gain.** 7 362 versus 7 306
  tokens; its extra derived obligations never change an answer.
- **No repair loop ran.** All 45 cells passed or failed on the first
  attempt. This trio cannot measure the feedback loop; a harder task is
  needed for that.

The next benchmark decision is therefore task selection, not N: the trio
is too easy to separate `contract`, `extract` and `full`. A task should
have a requirement whose hand contract adds a check the keyword
derivation misses, or the matrix should add a no-feedback arm (`high`
effort or a one-call mode) so the loop itself is measured.
